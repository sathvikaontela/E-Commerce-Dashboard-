
E-Commerce Sales & Customer Behaviour Dashboard

 👋 About the Project
This project dives into an e-commerce dataset to uncover what’s really happening behind the scenes — from what customers buy and when, to which products are returned and where sales are booming.

I used SQL to prepare and analyse the raw data, and built a fully interactive Power BI dashboard to visualize insights in a clear and actionable way.

 🧰 What I Used
- SQL for data cleaning, joins, aggregations, and return rate logic  
- Power BI for building dashboards with KPIs, charts, and filters  
- Excel (in some cases) for quick checks and calculations

 🔍 What You’ll Find Inside
 -📈 Monthly Sales Trends – See when people shop the most  
- 🏆 Top-Selling Products – Watches and smartphones are the stars  
- 🌎 Sales by Country – Japan, Egypt, and Australia lead the pack  
- 🔁 Return Analysis – Footwear and Home categories need some attention  
- 👥 Customer Segments – Most are Platinum buyers who shop frequently  
- ⏳ Order Gaps – UAE customers take the longest to reorder

 💡 Why It Matters
By combining SQL with visual storytelling in Power BI, I was able to answer real business questions like:
- Who are our best customers?
- What products should we promote more?
- Which countries need better marketing?
- Where are returns hurting our revenue?

And more importantly — what can we do about it.
